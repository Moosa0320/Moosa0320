### About

BS Software Engineering student at NASTP Institute of Information Technology, specializing in **AI and Cybersecurity**, graduating 2028. I design and build systems at the intersection of applied AI and security — from ML-driven diagnostics to intrusion detection and IoT-based physical security.

Beyond engineering, I'm Student Council President at NIIT, an Ambassador for the Centre of Excellence in Gaming & Animation (CEGA, Government of Pakistan), and a member of Bitcode Systems — a student community focused on AI/ML, IoT, and cybersecurity.

Open to internships and freelance engagements.
